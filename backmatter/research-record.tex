\chapter{Research and verification record}\label{app:record}
This appendix illustrates a transparent record of important research decisions.
Decide which records belong in the thesis and which should remain in a protected
research archive. Preserve confidentiality and explain restrictions on access.

\section{Claim and authority verification}
For each central claim, record the proposition, authority, exact pinpoint,
version and verification status. The example CSV is a working aid. A link that
opens successfully establishes retrieval; the relevance and force of the
authority require reading and analysis.

\section{Language and translation}
Identify the original language, the translation used, the translator and any
material uncertainty. Explain how legal terms were compared when a direct
linguistic equivalent would be misleading.

\section{Changes after assessment}
Keep a dated account of requested corrections and the version approved for
submission. Follow the applicable procedure for later substantive changes.
Groningen candidates should consult the specific approval constraints identified
in the repository's institutional guide.

\LawPrompt{Include only records appropriate for public disclosure. Keep raw
participant data, confidential correspondence and access credentials outside
the public repository and submission bundle.}
