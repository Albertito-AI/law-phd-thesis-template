# Submission checklist

Use a copy of this checklist in your private thesis project. Record the reviewer, date and evidence of completion. A green build establishes only the checks that were actually run.

## Argument and legal evidence

- [ ] The introduction asks a precise research question and explains the contribution.
- [ ] The method, scope, jurisdiction selection and legal cut-off are stated and justified.
- [ ] Each major conclusion follows from the analysis, with significant qualifications and counterarguments addressed.
- [ ] Every quotation has been checked against the original, including omissions, emphasis and translation.
- [ ] Important citations have verified source identity, exact pinpoints, applicable legal versions and subsequent treatment.
- [ ] Unresolved entries in the claim-evidence record have been resolved, qualified or removed.
- [ ] The bibliography and authority tables contain the intended entries, with consistent spelling and citation forms.

## Document and build

- [ ] Select the intended institutional profile and output mode.
- [ ] Build from a clean copy using the documented toolchain.
- [ ] Resolve missing citations, unresolved references, duplicate labels and material layout warnings.
- [ ] Search the source and final PDF for demonstration content, TODOs and unfilled placeholders.
- [ ] Inspect the title, contents, chapter openings, footnotes, authority tables, bibliography and final pages visually.
- [ ] Check long tables, quotations, URLs, non-English characters and font embedding.
- [ ] Compare the word-count categories with the university's actual counting rule.
- [ ] Verify the submitted PDF's metadata, bookmarks and hyperlinks.
- [ ] If an accessible or archival PDF standard is required, validate that standard with suitable tools and human review. Ordinary PDF compilation is not evidence of PDF/UA or PDF/A conformance.

## Rights, disclosure and privacy

- [ ] Record rights and permission for reused articles, figures and substantial third-party material.
- [ ] Identify co-author contributions and any previously published or submitted material.
- [ ] Complete any applicable ethics, data availability, funding and competing-interest statements.
- [ ] Review any AI-use or editorial-assistance disclosure against the institution's current rules. The optional [AI-use log](../examples/ai-use-log.csv) is a recording aid.
- [ ] Keep confidential evidence, participant data, identifying keys and credentials out of the public template repository.
- [ ] For an anonymous review copy, inspect the prose, acknowledgements, self-citations, PDF metadata, file names and embedded material manually. The `anonymous` mode cannot assess every identifying clue.
- [ ] Decide the thesis's copyright notice, deposit licence and any embargo separately from this template's MIT licence.

## Institutional and production approval

- [ ] Record the university rules and editions checked, plus any individual dispensation.
- [ ] Obtain approval of the actual title page and use the approved PDF where required.
- [ ] Confirm the required declarations, translated material and accompanying documents.
- [ ] Obtain supervisory and examination approvals before treating the thesis as final.
- [ ] Confirm paper dimensions, margins, binding, colour and print specification with the printer.
- [ ] Review the printer's proof and record the authorised final file.
- [ ] Check deposit deadlines, required copies, access conditions and the exact files uploaded.
- [ ] Preserve a dated submission record and a checksum of the final PDF.

Groningen students should also complete the [Groningen-specific checks](groningen.md), including translated title/subtitle and summary, propositions, approved title page and the applicable final-version requirements.
