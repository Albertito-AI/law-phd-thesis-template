# Groningen profile

Sources checked on **8 November 2026**. This is an independently maintained starting point. Your Graduate School, approved title page and applicable university requirements determine the submission you may make.

## Choose the right output

Use the `groningen` profile for the institutional starting point and select the appropriate `review`, `anonymous` or `print` mode. An anonymous review copy is an editorial convenience. It does not replace the named thesis required for formal submission.

The October 2026 guidance describes **170 × 240 mm** as the standard booklet size while allowing other sizes. Use those explicit dimensions when preparing that format. The label “B5” in the guidance should not be translated automatically into LaTeX's ISO `b5paper` setting, which has different dimensions. Agree the trim size, binding allowance and production specification with the printer. A4 remains useful for working copies. [G1]

## Front matter and accompanying material

| Item | Action for a Groningen thesis |
| --- | --- |
| Official title page | Obtain the current university template, complete it and secure approval through Hora Finita. Insert the approved PDF in the template's approved-title-PDF slot. Check that its wording and appearance survive inclusion. |
| Translated title and summary | For an English thesis, provide the Dutch title, subtitle where applicable, and scientific summary as a separate appendix. For a Dutch thesis, provide the corresponding English material. |
| Propositions | Prepare at least six academically defensible propositions. Use `propositions.tex` for the separate accompanying document and obtain the required supervisory approval. |
| Main structure | Include the research question, academic analysis, conclusion, abstract and contents. Adapt the chapter sequence to the argument. |
| Publication-based thesis | Explain how the chapters form a coherent thesis, identify individual contributions and record the necessary permissions for third-party rights. |
| CV and acknowledgements | Keep editable sections available. The October guidance expects these in the final version; the March Regulations describe the CV as optional. Confirm the applicable expectation with the Graduate School. |

The translated-title requirement, separate appendix, propositions and publication-based route are addressed in Articles 4.2–4.5 of the March 2026 Regulations. [G2]

## Before printing and depositing

The official ceremony page requires the title page to be submitted at least two months before the defence. Follow its current single, double or joint doctorate template as appropriate. A title page generated for demonstration by this project is not evidence of approval. [G3]

Record the assessment decision, approved changes and the versions sent to the printer and repository. Recheck the university's submission timeline before fixing production dates. It provides separate milestones for publicity, printed copies and electronic deposit. [G4]

Decide the thesis rights and embargo position with the appropriate university service. A template licence does not grant rights in incorporated articles, photographs, interview material or other third-party work. Maintain a permissions record and make the required contribution disclosures. The Library's publishing guidance explains deposit, licensing and embargo routes. [G5]

Use the AI-use log to record any relevant assistance. The current formatting guidance asks for the tool, purpose, prompts, affected material and date. Keep sensitive prompts in an approved private record and seek institutional advice about an appropriate disclosure where confidentiality is involved. [G1]
