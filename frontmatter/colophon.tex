\noindent\textbf{About this working example}\par\medskip
LexThesis is an independently authored, open template for doctoral legal research.
The example demonstrates layout, legal citation mechanics and research records.
Replace its instructional text and demonstration metadata with your own work.
The repository documentation identifies the sources consulted and the limits of
its institutional and citation support.
\par\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Rights and publication}\par
The MIT licence covers the original template code and guidance. Your thesis,
third-party publications, figures and research data retain their own rights.
Choose the licence for your thesis separately, with your university and any
relevant publishers. Record permissions and exceptions in the final colophon.
\par\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Version}\par
LexThesis 1.0.0. Source review: 8 November 2026.
\par\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Accessibility}\par
This baseline provides selectable text, document metadata, navigable headings
and hyperlinks. Formal PDF/UA or PDF/A conformance has not been established.
