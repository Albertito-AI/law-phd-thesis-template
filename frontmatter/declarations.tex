\addchap{Research declarations}
\LawPrompt{Complete the declarations required by your institution. State only
what is true of your own work. Obtain approved wording where required.}
\section*{Authorship and contributions}
Describe responsibility for the research and writing, earlier publications,
co-authored chapters and the contributions of collaborators.
\section*{Ethics, confidentiality and data}
Record applicable ethics approvals, consent arrangements and restrictions on
access. Explain how protected material is handled. A doctrinal project should
explain its own position without adopting a fictional ethics approval.
\section*{Tools and assistance}
Describe material research, translation, analytical and writing assistance.
Where generative artificial intelligence was used, record the tool, date,
task, affected text and checks performed, together with any prompts required
by your institution. The example log is supplied separately.
\section*{Funding and competing interests}
Record support and interests relevant to the research. Explain any influence
on research design, source selection or publication.
